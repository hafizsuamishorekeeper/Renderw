In 1932, the Australian army went to war with a bird. And lost.

Western Australia. Thousands of war veterans were farming wheat on dry, difficult land. Then about twenty thousand hungry emus marched in and started wrecking the crops.

The farmers begged for help. So the government sent a major, two soldiers, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets. The plan was simple: finish it in days.

But the moment the guns opened fire, the emus scattered. They split into small groups and vanished into the bush. The soldiers chased them. The birds were faster.

So the major mounted a gun on a truck. But the ground was so bumpy, nobody could aim. And the truck couldn't keep up anyway.

Weeks passed. Newspapers started laughing. Politicians started asking questions.

Finally, the army packed up and quietly went home. Even the major admitted he was impressed.

Final score: the army, zero. The emus, undefeated.
